# Fonts

Alternative fonts, kept for a future font option in Settings. None of them is
loaded by the game yet (the game font is `../m6x11plus.woff2`). Files are as
downloaded from DaFont on 2026-10-28.

| Folder | Font | Author | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `grape-soda/` | Grape Soda | jeti | [DaFont](https://www.dafont.com/grapesoda-2.font), [fontenddev.com](https://fontenddev.com/fonts/grape-soda/) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): free for personal and commercial use, credit required |
| `cutepixel/` | CutePixel | zK4rim | [DaFont](https://www.dafont.com/cutepixel.font) | 100% free for personal and commercial use; credit appreciated |
| `superstar/` | Superstar | memesbruh03 (Aaron D. Chand) | [DaFont](https://www.dafont.com/superstar-2.font), [memesbruh03.neocities.org](https://memesbruh03.neocities.org/) | 100% free for personal and commercial use (see `superstar_info.txt`) |
| `digital-disco/` | Digital Disco (and Thin) | jeti | [DaFont](https://www.dafont.com/digital-disco.font), [fontenddev.com](https://fontenddev.com/fonts/digital-disco/) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): free for personal and commercial use, credit required |

Notes:

- The authors' own words, from their DaFont pages:
  - Grape Soda and Digital Disco: "You are free to use this font for personal or
    commercial projects, all I ask is that you include credit."
  - CutePixel: "This font 100% free for personal and commercial use. Any credits is appreciated."
- Superstar ships two files. Its author recommends the MB03 version
  (`superstar_memesbruh03.ttf`); the "orig" one (`superstarorig_memesbruh03.ttf`)
  says "Original design by someone else", so use the MB03 one.
- When a font is added to the game, credit it in the README's Credits and in the
  game's credits, and convert it to `.woff2` for the web.
